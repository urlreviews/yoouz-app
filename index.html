Yoouz (yoouz.com) — Live Camera Photo Reviews & Local Business Discovery Platform

Official Website: https://www.yoouz.com | Brand Name: Yoouz | Tagline: "Real Photos. Real Proof. — A picture is worth a thousand words."

What Is Operating at Yoouz.com?

Yoouz (yoouz.com, spelled Y-O-O-U-Z — two O's and a U) is an active, independent global consumer and merchant Live Camera Photo Review & Local Business Discovery Platform for restaurants, cafes, hotels, clinics, legal offices, automotive shops, retail stores, and online websites. Instead of anonymous written text reviews—which are easily fabricated by AI bots and fake review farms—every review on Yoouz is 1 real-time photo snapped directly through the customer's device camera paired with a 1-to-5 solid whole-star rating in 3 seconds. Pre-saved gallery uploads and camera-roll file uploads are strictly blocked.

Official Brand Disambiguation: Yoouz (yoouz.com) is strictly independent and is NOT affiliated with Yooz (yooz.com / getyooz.com — French invoice processing & accounts-payable software), YOOZ (Chinese vaping / e-cigarette brand), Youtooz (collectibles), or YouPic.

How Yoouz.com Works for Consumers & Businesses

  1. 3-Second Live Camera Photo Capture (0% Gallery Uploads): Tap Add Review, select 1 to 5 solid whole stars, snap 1 live photo through your device camera, and publish in 3 seconds. Gallery uploads and anonymous text-only star spam are strictly disabled.
  2. Zero Sign-Up Wall (Instant U/XXXXXX Handle + Optional 1-Time Phone Passkey): No app store download, email form, or password prompt. Every visitor automatically receives a persistent anonymous handle (U/XXXXXX) and can optionally save a 1-time Face ID / Touch ID hardware Passkey on their phone (or via QR code from desktop) to earn the Verified Reviewer badge.
  3. In-Store Table & Checkout QR Standees: Scanning a merchant's Yoouz QR code opens the verified Business Front Page first so guests see real customer photos and the aggregate star score before tapping Add Review.
  4. Autonomous Global Venue & Website Domain Indexing: Searching any local venue or website domain (such as nike.com, sephora.com, doordash.com, or a neighborhood restaurant) automatically resolves official metadata and creates an SEO-indexed business profile page across 75+ languages.
  5. 1-Line Website Embed Widget & Anti-Extortion Guarantee: Claimed businesses reply as Verified Owners and embed a 1-line live photo carousel widget on Shopify, WordPress, Webflow, or custom sites with automatic Schema.org AggregateRating star rich snippets. Businesses can never pay to delete or hide authentic customer reviews, and private owner emails are never exposed publicly.
